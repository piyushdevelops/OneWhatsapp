Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 9, 2026

OneOperations operates a WhatsApp-based customer communication platform for The June Shop. This page explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when customers interact with us through WhatsApp, website forms, or support channels.

Information We Collect

  • Customer contact details such as name, phone number, and optional email address.
  • Messages exchanged with us on WhatsApp, including support and order-related conversations.
  • Basic order and service context used to respond to customer questions and requests.
  • Technical logs needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the platform.

How We Use Information

  • To send and receive customer support messages and order updates.
  • To respond to inquiries, resolve issues, and improve customer experience.
  • To operate automations, message templates, and workflow tools used by our business.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

How Information Is Shared

We only share information with service providers and platforms required to operate our customer messaging workflows, including hosting infrastructure and Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. We do not sell customer personal information.

Data Retention

We retain customer communication data only for as long as needed for support, operations, compliance, and record-keeping purposes. We may delete or anonymize information when it is no longer required.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect the information we process. No system can guarantee absolute security, but we work to safeguard customer data appropriately.

Your Choices

Customers may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable law and operational requirements.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact The June Shop support team through the business contact channel you already use with us.

Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, the revised version will be posted at this same URL with an updated effective date.
